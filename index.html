Patterns worth tracking

Updated as new walks and free-work transcripts are added. These are working observations, not diagnoses. I’ll revise them when later records support, qualify or contradict a pattern.

🏡 Wanting to leave the park

Across several walks, Nova has sometimes wanted to head home after toileting, after the poo bag was binned, or when near a bench. The sequence recurs, but the precise cause is unclear. Keep tracking whether it happens on walks where these events do not occur too.

Children and changing distance

Responses to children and other people vary with context. Nova has managed some people at a distance without an obvious reaction, but has also growled or lunged when a child was closer. Log distance, direction of approach, lead status and recovery, without assuming one factor explains the reaction.

🐕 Tail wagging is not enough on its own

Tail position and wagging have not always matched the rest of Nova’s behaviour. A wag has co-occurred with growling or lunging, so interpret it alongside posture, vocalisations, movement, distance and how quickly she recovers.
